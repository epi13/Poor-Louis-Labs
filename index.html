About

Poor Louis Labs explores practical computing in three threads:

  • Tractability & Branchless Design — tools that replace control flow with masks, muxes, and arithmetic.
  • Reversible Systems — RCR-style witnesses, undoable memory, and time‑travel debugging.
  • Digital Senses — turning intrinsic machine noise (timing jitter, thermal drift) into usable signals.

We publish essays, code, and open lab notes so others can build faster than we did.
