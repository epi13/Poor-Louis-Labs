Poor Louis Labs

Welcome to Poor Louis Labs, our humble little corner of curiosity; 
an Alaskan R&D outpost exploring tractable computing, educational systems, 
digital senses, and the craft of making better tools.

🧠 What We Do (Kind Of)
We don’t really stay in one lane. Poor Louis Labs is about:
⦁	Inventing oddball tools and useful things that might work.
⦁	Blending code, nature, art, and science without asking for permission.
⦁	Testing theories. Challenging assumptions. Following hunches.
⦁	Taking inspiration from Alaska, from old notebooks, from kids' questions, from the stars.

🔬 Current Main Project
Right now, the main focus is on MAoD (Modular Arithmetic-only DSL) 
— a sprawling experiment in reversible, branchless programming built from scratch in Zig.
It’s mathy. It’s weird. It’s delightful. And it might just change how we think about computation.

💡 Philosophy
We believe good ideas can come from anywhere 
— a trail walk, a poem, a voltage spike, a tea leaf. 
Poor Louis Labs is a place to hold those ideas up to the light.
We don’t rush.
We don’t pitch decks.
We just build and share.

Thanks for stopping by. ✨
